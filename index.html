About

I am Anlin Li (李岸霖), and you can also call me Napert. I am a cancer immunology and lung cancer researcher/student at Sun Yat-sen University Cancer Center (中山大学肿瘤防治中心).

I began working in multi-omics bioinformatics for cancer immunology in 2017, during my first year of medical school. During my master’s training in the Department of Medical Oncology at Sun Yat-sen University Cancer Center, I focused on omics-based bioinformatics research in lung cancer immunology.

For my M.D. training and future research, I aim to focus on patient populations for whom current immunotherapies are not yet applicable or effective, particularly patients with immune-infiltrated “hot” tumors that respond poorly to immunotherapy. Guided by large-scale omics analyses, I seek to identify and experimentally validate dimensions of cancer–immune vulnerability, independent of established frameworks, that reflect evolutionarily dominant biology.

My current project focuses on mechanisms of immune escape in NK-cell-enriched tumors.
